Objective: implement the incomplete flows and pending hardening items of the MagniFit audit artifact (source: `docs/flujos-usuario.md`, `odd/tasks/hardening-seo-flows.md`). Base: `feat/user-flows` at the commit that adds this file, stacked on `feat/hardening-seo-flows`.
